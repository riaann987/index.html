📘 What is ReadSpark?

ReadSpark is a student-focused reading platform that motivates young readers through personalized content, reading challenges, credits, and leaderboards. It makes reading engaging, interactive, and rewarding.

✨ Key Features

Personalized Reading

Content tailored by reading level and genre preferences.

Credit System

Earn credits by reading daily, completing challenges, and quizzes.

Challenges & Streaks

Weekly and monthly reading goals to build consistency.

Leaderboards

Rankings for most books read, highest streaks, and advanced readers.
